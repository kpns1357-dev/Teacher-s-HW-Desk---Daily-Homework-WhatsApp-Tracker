================================================================================
               TEACHER'S QUICK GUIDE: DAILY HOMEWORK & WHATSAPP DESK
================================================================================

Website Link:
https://kpns1357-dev.github.io/Teacher-s-HW-Desk---Daily-Homework-WhatsApp-Tracker/

(Tip: On phone browser, tap menu -> "Add to Home Screen" to use like a mobile app)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: ADD YOUR BATCH & LINK YOUR WHATSAPP GROUP (One-Time Setup)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Tap the "+ Add Batch" button on the main screen.
2. Enter the Batch Name (e.g. Class 8th, Class 9th Maths, Class 10th Science).

3. IMPORTANT: ADD YOUR WHATSAPP GROUP INVITE LINK
   - Open WhatsApp on your phone.
   - Go to your batch group (e.g., Class 8th Homework group).
   - Tap the group name at the top to open Group Info.
   - Scroll down and tap "Invite to group via link" -> "Copy link".
   - Paste that link into the "WhatsApp Group Invite Link" field.
   
   Why do this?
   When you finish checking homework, the app will automatically copy the report
   and open this exact group directly on your phone/PC without searching contacts!

4. Paste your student names (one name per line).
5. Click "Create Batch".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CHECK DAILY HOMEWORK (< 30 Seconds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Tap on the batch you want to check.
2. For each student, tap their status:
   - [Done]       : Green checkmark (Completed)
   - [Incomplete] : Amber warning icon (tap the pencil icon to add a note
                    e.g. "Only questions 1-3 done", "Rough notebook")
   - [Not Done]   : Red cross (Did not do homework)
   - [Absent]     : Grey icon (Student was absent today)

   * Quick Tip: If most students finished, tap "Mark All Done" at the top,
     then only tap the few who did not do it!

3. Holiday or Test Day:
   If there is no homework today, simply tap "No Homework" or "Holiday" at the top.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: SEND TO WHATSAPP WITH 1 TAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the green button at the bottom: "Open Batch Group & Paste".
2. The app will:
   - Auto-copy the entire formatted summary to your clipboard.
   - Open the WhatsApp group directly.
3. Tap "Paste" and click "Send".

Done! All records automatically save in the background.
